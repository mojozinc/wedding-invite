A Decade of Fond Acquaintance

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are quick answers to the most common queries.

What is the dress code for each ceremony?

We invite you to wear sunny florals for the Haldi, sparkling Indo-Western / festive outfits for Sangeet, traditional royal Indian attire for the Pheras, and formal evening wear for the Grand Reception!

What is your gift policy?

Your loving presence, blessings, and energetic dance moves are the greatest gifts we could ever ask for! If you still wish to gift something, we kindly request only your warm blessings or contributing to our honeymoon travel fund.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely! Children are very much part of our joyful celebration. We will also have kid-friendly mocktails, sweet treats, and play corners available.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Yes! Please snap away and tag your memories with our official wedding hashtag #SandeepLovesShraddha so we can relive every moment together.

Who can I contact for urgent travel or stay assistance?

Our wedding concierge helpline will be active 24/7 starting November 1st. Reach out at hello@sandeeplovesshraddha.com or WhatsApp our coordinator team.
